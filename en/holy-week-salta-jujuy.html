Holy Week in the Quebrada de Humahuaca

The Virgin of Punta Corral comes down to Tilcara

In the Quebrada the week has its own calendar of faith. This was the 2026 program published by Jujuy's tourism office; the 2027 program is not out yet. If the pattern repeats, the descent to Tilcara would be on Wednesday, March 24, 2027.

Palm Sunday

From 7:00 a.m., the image of the Virgin of Punta Corral is carried down from her mountain sanctuary to the village of Tumbaya.

Holy Wednesday

At 5:00 a.m. the descent toward Tilcara begins with sikuri bands (Andean panpipe bands) and pilgrims. In 2026 the image reached the church square in Tilcara at 8:30 p.m.

Good Friday

At nightfall, the Stations of the Cross through Tilcara's ermitas, the shrines the village sets up as the stations of the procession.

What it means for your trip

The processions are public, and Tilcara fills with pilgrims and visitors. With a driver you do not have to find parking or drive back to your hotel at night.

Frequently asked questions

What travelers ask about Holy Week

How far ahead should we book?

As soon as you have dates. In 2026 the main destinations of Salta were at 82.5% occupancy over the long weekend, and vehicles with drivers are booked for the same days.

Can we watch the procession in Tilcara?

Yes, it is public. We plan the day around the times Jujuy's tourism office publishes for 2027, and agree a meeting point with you for the ride back.
