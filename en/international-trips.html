How it works

What changes when the trip crosses a border

  • A permit for every trip. Each trip abroad needs its own permit from the CNRT, Argentina's national transport regulator, filed at least 72 hours before departure with the passenger list. That is why we ask for each traveler's full name and passport or ID number, and why booking at least a week ahead is best.
  • Insurance extended to the destination country. The vehicle travels with the cover the international permit requires.
  • Each passenger's documents. Argentines and citizens of Mercosur and associated countries cross into Chile and Bolivia with their ID card; everyone else with a passport and, depending on nationality, a visa. Each destination page has the details.
  • Border and road status. We check the day before and again before leaving. If a pass is closed, we tell you and reschedule with you.

Official sources, checked Oct 10, 2026: occasional trips abroad (CNRT), border crossings and their status and documents by neighboring country (Argentine government).
