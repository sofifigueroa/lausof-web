Units fitted out for mine-site entry

Buggy whip, strobe beacon, fire extinguishers, a second spare wheel and snow chains on the units assigned to that work.

On-board trip log per unit

Every trip is recorded.

Incident reports signed by the driver

Every incident is documented, without exception.

Zero tolerance

Zero alcohol and no drugs at the wheel. Smoke-free vehicles.

Documented preventive maintenance

In our own workshop, not subcontracted.

Documents

Documents for contractors' vehicles and crew

Lausof's office uploads each contractor's vehicle and crew papers to the mines' portals and keeps them current, using the access the contractor provides.

Included:

  • Uploading and renewing each vehicle's papers: insurance and proof of payment, the waiver-of-subrogation clauses each mine asks for, technical inspection (RTO) and registration card (cédula).
  • Uploading and renewing the papers of crew who enter the mine: workers' compensation (ART) certificate, form 931 and its receipt, and driver's license.
  • Advance notice of anything about to expire.
  • Requests for missing papers to the contractor, its insurance broker or its ART.
  • Correcting and resubmitting anything a portal rejects or flags.

Portals the office works in: SICOP · Exactian · Minexus.

Trademarks of their respective owners, not affiliated with Lausof; named to show where the office works.

Billed as a monthly fee, quoted on the number of vehicles and people.
