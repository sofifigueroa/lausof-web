The service

4x4 pickups by the month, self-drive, for your operation

We have worked in the Puna, the Andean high plateau of northwest Argentina, for more than a decade: we know the gravel roads, the altitude and what they demand of a pickup. We rent out the same 4x4 pickups we run in that operation, maintained by the same mechanics.

Mining companies and contractors

4x4 pickups to work in the Puna, from a company that knows the mine-site entry requirements.

Service companies

Continuous mobility for crews and supervisors working outside the city, without tying up capital in a fleet of your own.

Construction and assembly projects

Pickups for the length of the job: you rent by the month and hand the unit back when the project ends.

Agencies and consulting firms

Field surveys and work on mountain roads, with units prepared for that ground.

The units

4x4 pickups maintained in our own workshop

Our workshop was built to keep our own fleet running: the pickups are inspected and repaired by our own mechanics. Fewer days off the road after a breakdown.

On our own mining work the 4x4 pickups carry the equipment asked for at the gate: roll cage, buggy whip with flag, strobe beacon, fire extinguishers, a second spare wheel and snow chains. Entry requirements differ from one operation to the next, so we prepare the unit to what yours asks for.

Frequently asked questions

What companies ask before renting

Is the rental with or without a driver?

Without a driver, on a monthly basis: the unit is at your company's disposal and your authorized drivers operate it. If what you need is the trip handled for you, that is a different service: see mining logistics or personnel transport.

Who does the maintenance?

We do, in our own workshop in Salta and with our own mechanics. Maintenance is included in the rental: the unit is inspected on the agreed plan and you do not have to arrange anything with third parties.

What is needed to rent?

A company with a current CUIT, a signed rental contract with the conditions of use, and the corresponding insurance coverage in force. Tell us your case by email or WhatsApp and we will confirm the steps.

Where is the unit handed over?

Delivery and return are coordinated in Salta, on the dates and at the point agreed in the contract.

Do you issue Factura A?

Yes. Our legal name is Lausof S.R.L., CUIT 30-71146774-9, and we issue Factura A, the Argentine tax invoice.

Can we deal with you in English?

Yes, by email and by WhatsApp: we reply in English, normally the same business day (Argentina time, UTC−3). Telephone calls are answered in Spanish.

Do you need a 4x4 pickup for your operation?

Tell us how long you need it for and where it will be driven. We reply with the quote in writing, with the conditions clear.
