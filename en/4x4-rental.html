4x4 pickup rental

4x4 pickup rental in Salta

4x4 pickups with a driver for travelers, and monthly rental for companies and mining contractors, with or without a driver. Units prepared for gravel roads and altitude, maintained in our own workshop, for the Puna, Route 40 or a trip into Chile or Bolivia.

The service

A 4x4 pickup for your trip or for your operation

We have worked in the Puna, the Andean high plateau of northwest Argentina, for more than a decade: we know the gravel roads, the altitude and what they demand of a pickup. We offer the same 4x4 pickups we run in that operation, maintained by the same mechanics, with a driver for travelers and by the month for teams working in the region.

Travelers, with a driver

A 4x4 with one of our drivers, who knows the high-altitude roads, to explore the north at your own pace: the Puna, Route 40, the Salinas Grandes salt flats, or a trip to San Pedro de Atacama (Chile) or Uyuni (Bolivia).

Mining companies and contractors

Monthly 4x4 pickup rental, with or without a driver, to work in the Puna, from a company that knows the mine-site entry requirements. It is a contract service for companies.

Companies, projects and agencies

Mobility for crews, supervisors and field work, by the month and with or without a driver, without tying up capital in a fleet of your own.

The units

4x4 pickups maintained in our own workshop

Our workshop was built to keep our own fleet running: the pickups are inspected and repaired by our own mechanics. Fewer days off the road after a breakdown.

On our own mining work the 4x4 pickups carry the equipment asked for at the gate: roll cage, buggy whip with flag, strobe beacon, fire extinguishers, a second spare wheel and snow chains. Entry requirements differ from one operation to the next, so we equip the unit to your site's requirements.

For travelers

With a driver, into Chile and Bolivia too

For travelers, the pickup always comes with one of our drivers, who knows the gravel roads, the altitude and the border passes.

Into Chile or Bolivia

We drive you to San Pedro de Atacama, through the Jama or the Sico pass, and to Uyuni, through La Quiaca and Villazón. As the owner of the pickup, we arrange the authorization for it to leave the country; that is why the pass and the dates are set when you book.

Documents for the border

You do not need a driver's license: our driver is at the wheel. What each border asks for, with its official sources, is on Salta to San Pedro de Atacama and Salta to Uyuni.

Want to drive yourself?

We do not rent self-drive pickups to tourists. If you want to drive, or need to drop the vehicle off in another city, we can recommend licensed rental companies in Salta, or do the same trip with a driver.

What is included

What the rental includes

The price is quoted on the term, the route and, on a monthly company rental, whether it comes with a driver. The quote goes out in writing, with the conditions clear from the first day.

Maintenance included

Preventive and corrective work in Salta, by the mechanics who service our own fleet.

GPS tracking

GPS on the units in service, for the length of the rental.

Connectivity where there is no coverage

For destinations with no mobile signal, Starlink satellite connectivity can be added on request.

Starting from Salta

Trips with a driver start in Salta, from the place we agree. On a monthly rental, we coordinate delivery and return of the pickup in Salta.

Conditions in writing

Term, distance allowance in kilometers and conditions of use are set out in the contract, with no hidden costs.

How to book

From inquiry to handover, in four steps

1. Your inquiry

An email or WhatsApp message with your dates, your route, how many passengers and whether you will cross into Chile or Bolivia. For a company, the rental term and whether you want a driver.

2. The quote

We reply in English with the quote in writing and the rental conditions.

3. Confirmation

The service is confirmed in writing. If you will cross a border, we prepare the exit authorization. On a monthly rental, the company signs the contract and sends the details of everyone who will drive.

4. On the road

On the agreed day, the pickup leaves Salta inspected in our workshop and ready for the road.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask before renting

Is the rental with or without a driver?

For travelers, always with a driver: one of our drivers, who knows the gravel and high-altitude roads, comes with it. For companies and mining contractors, the monthly rental can be with or without a driver; without one, the pickup is driven by the company's drivers named in the contract.

Do you rent self-drive pickups to tourists?

No. We do not rent self-drive pickups to tourists, from abroad or from Argentina, and we do not do one-way self-drive rentals. We can recommend licensed rental companies in Salta, or do the same trip with one of our drivers.

Can we travel into Chile or Bolivia in the pickup?

Yes, with one of our drivers. Tell us when you book, with the border pass and the dates, because we arrange the authorization for the pickup to leave the country, as its owner.

Who does the maintenance?

We do, in our own workshop in Salta and with our own mechanics. Maintenance is included in the rental: the pickup is inspected before every handover and, on long rentals, on the agreed plan.

Where does the trip start?

Trips with a driver start in Salta, from the place we agree. On a monthly company rental, delivery and return are coordinated in Salta, at the place and on the dates agreed in the contract.

Do you issue Factura A?

Yes. Our legal name is Lausof S.R.L., CUIT 30-71146774-9, and we issue Factura A, the Argentine tax invoice.

Can we deal with you in English?

Yes, by email and by WhatsApp: we reply in English, normally the same business day (Argentina time, UTC−3). Telephone calls are answered in Spanish.

Do you need a 4x4 pickup for your trip or your operation?

Tell us your dates, your route and, for a company, the rental term. We reply with the quote in writing, with the conditions clear.
