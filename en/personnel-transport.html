The service

Minibuses with a driver for companies and institutions

Passenger transport was our first service. We started in 2010 and we still run it every day, with drivers who have worked the roads of Salta and northwest Argentina for years.

Companies

Daily personnel transport: fixed routes, rotating shifts and on-time departures.

Construction sites

The whole team to the site in the same minibus, on a route built around the working day.

Clinics and health centers

Healthcare staff on rotating shifts: the route follows every shift change.

Schools and institutions

Transport for schools and institutions, with a professional driver and a licensed unit.

Mine sites and contractors

Crew changes and camp runs for mining projects and the contractors working for them. The full high-altitude picture is on mining logistics in the Puna; construction-phase crews on crew transport for lithium projects.

Minibus rental with driver

Need a minibus for one job rather than a standing service? We also rent 19-seat minibuses with a driver to companies and for events. For visitors and groups on the classic routes, see tours and private transfers.

Visitors and delegations

Site visits, conferences and executive transfers

When the trip is a one-off (a conference, a visiting delegation, a group crossing into another province), we put the whole job together: unit, driver and route, with the times agreed in advance.

Executive visits

We meet the group on arrival at Salta's Martín Miguel de Güemes airport and cover the transfers for the whole stay, with a single contact for changes to the agenda.

Conferences and conventions

Attendee transfers between hotels, venues and event dinners, coordinated with the organizer's schedule.

Corporate events

Launches, training sessions and year-end parties: the whole group arrives and leaves together.

Delegations

Delegations visiting Salta or touring several cities: the minibus stays with the group for the whole agenda.

Salta, Jujuy and Tucumán

Interprovincial trips under our CNRT license: crossing a provincial border with the paperwork in order.

Same-day return trips

Early departure, a working day at destination and back. The driver waits, so the group does not depend on anyone else's timetable.

The units

19-seat minibuses maintained in our own workshop

Our workshop was set up to maintain the fleet: the minibuses are inspected and repaired by our own mechanics, so mechanical problems are fixed in-house, without waiting on outside shops.

How we work

A family company running the whole operation

No call center: someone from the company answers.

Fixed or on-demand routes

Daily routes, rotating shifts or one-off trips: the service is built around your operation.

Current licenses

CNRT license No. 60284 for interprovincial work, and an AMT permit for passenger transport within Salta province.

Professional drivers

Licensed for the vehicle class, with a current medical fitness certificate (psicofísico) and workers' compensation insurance (ART).

Documented trips

GPS on the units in service and an on-board trip log for each unit: every trip is on record.

For your procurement team

Why hire a licensed carrier

When your people travel every day, what matters is not only the minibus: it is what stands behind it. We work with a current CNRT license and AMT permit, passenger insurance in force, and drivers holding a professional license, a current medical fitness certificate and workers' compensation cover. We hand that documentation over whenever a procurement or HSE team asks for it, including for a prequalification pack.

Our legal name is Lausof S.R.L., our CUIT (Argentine tax ID) is 30-71146774-9, and we issue Factura A, the Argentine tax invoice, with the details a supplier registration form asks for. If your procurement team needs anything else, write to transporte@lausof.com.

Questions

What companies ask us before hiring

How many seats do the minibuses have?

Nineteen passengers plus the driver. The service always comes with a professional driver.

Do you run fixed routes as well as one-off trips?

Yes. We build daily fixed routes, cover rotating shifts, and also run one-off trips on demand.

Can we rent a minibus with a driver for an event?

Yes. Tell us the date, how many people are traveling and the route, and we come back with a written quote.

Does the driver stay with the group during an event?

Yes. For conferences, delegations and site visits the unit can stay with the group for the day or for the whole stay, with the transfers in between included in the quote.

Do you run same-day trips to Jujuy or Tucumán?

Yes. Our CNRT license is interprovincial, so we run same-day return trips between Salta, Jujuy and Tucumán, with the driver waiting for the group at destination.

Can you also move our company vehicles?

We coordinate vehicle transfers for companies and mining operations, quoted on request. Tell us the type of vehicle, where it is and where it has to go, and we send you a quote.

Do you issue Factura A?

Yes. We are Lausof S.R.L., CUIT 30-71146774-9, and we issue Factura A.

Do you need to move your people?

To get a useful quote in one round, include: the service; the dates; origin and destination; how many passengers are traveling; and your company and a contact. We reply with a written quote.
