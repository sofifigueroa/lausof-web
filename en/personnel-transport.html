Construction sites

Clinics and health centers

Schools and institutions

Transport for schools and institutions, with a professional driver and a licensed unit.

Mine sites and contractors

Crew changes and camp runs for mining projects and the contractors working for them. The full high-altitude picture is on mining logistics in the Puna.

Minibus rental with driver

Need a minibus for one job rather than a standing service? We also rent 19-seat minibuses with a driver to companies and for events.

Visitors and delegations

Site visits, conferences and executive transfers

Executive visits

Conferences and conventions

Attendee transfers between hotels, venues and event dinners, coordinated with the organizer's schedule.

Corporate events

Delegations

Salta, Jujuy and Tucumán

Interprovincial trips under our CNRT license: crossing a provincial border with the paperwork in order.

Same-day return trips

Early departure, a working day at destination and back. The driver waits, so the group does not depend on anyone else's timetable.

Questions

What companies ask us before hiring

How many seats do the minibuses have?

Nineteen passengers plus the driver. The service always comes with a professional driver.

Do you run fixed routes as well as one-off trips?

Yes. We build daily fixed routes, cover rotating shifts, and also run one-off trips on demand.

Can we rent a minibus with a driver for an event?

Yes. Tell us the date, how many people are traveling and the route, and we come back with a quote the same business day.

Does the driver stay with the group during an event?

Yes. For conferences, delegations and site visits the unit can stay with the group for the day or for the whole stay, with the transfers in between included in the quote.

Do you run same-day trips to Jujuy or Tucumán?

Yes. Our CNRT license is interprovincial, so we run same-day return trips between Salta, Jujuy and Tucumán, with the driver waiting for the group at destination.

Do you issue Factura A?

Yes. We are Lausof S.R.L., CUIT 30-71146774-9, and we issue Factura A.

Do you need to move your people?

To get a useful quote in one round, include: the service; the dates; origin and destination; how many passengers are traveling; and your company and a contact. We reply the same business day (Argentina time).
