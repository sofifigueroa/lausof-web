Who it is for

Operators, agencies and hotels selling northwest Argentina

We are a licensed travel agency ourselves and have worked with other agencies for years. We know what an inbound program looks like: the timing, the stops that are worth making, and what it means to be at the hotel on time. We operate all year, not only in high season.

Foreign tour operators and DMCs

Itineraries through Salta, the Quebrada de Humahuaca, the Puna, San Pedro de Atacama or Uyuni that need ground transport with a driver.

Special-interest tours

Photography, birding and nature groups, traveling with the guide or specialist your company sends.

Local inbound agencies

Vehicles with a driver for your regular departures, and for the groups that do not fit your own vehicles.

Agencies elsewhere in Argentina

Groups flying in from Buenos Aires, Córdoba or the rest of the country: we meet them at the airport and drive the full program.

Hotels and concierge desks

Airport-to-hotel transfers for your guests and the day trips your front desk offers.

Events and weddings

Guests of a wedding or a conference your agency organizes. See wedding transportation.

On your behalf

Your clients travel with you; we drive

You sell the trip, and that is how your clients experience it. The driver meets the group with your agency’s or your hotel’s name sign, follows the program you built and handles what comes up on the road. Any change to the program is agreed with you, not with your clients.

We take care of the part that takes the most work in the north: a vehicle fit for gravel and altitude, a driver who knows the road, departure times, and the paperwork for trips across provincial lines or abroad.

  • The driver meets the group with your agency’s or your hotel’s name sign
  • Your agency sets the program, the stops and the times
  • One contact on your side and one on ours, by WhatsApp or email, for the whole trip
  • A passenger list with names and ID numbers before each departure
  • 19-seat minibuses and 4x4 pickups; for larger groups we add vehicles to the same program
  • The driver knows the road but is not a licensed tour guide. Your agency provides the guide

Frequently asked questions

What operators ask before working with us

Do you work under our agency’s name?

Yes. The driver meets your clients with your agency’s or your hotel’s name sign and follows the program you built. Changes to the program are agreed with you.

Do you work on commission or net rates?

Commercial terms are agreed with each agency and each hotel. Write to us and we will work them out before the first departure.

Can you cross into Chile and Bolivia?

Yes. Under our CNRT license No. 60284 we drive between provinces, and we run trips to Chile and Bolivia. Each passenger travels with the documents each country requires.

Is the driver a guide?

No. The driver knows the roads and the stops, but is not a licensed tour guide. Your agency provides the guide.

When is the busiest season?

For foreign visitors to Salta, October and November; for domestic travelers, the July winter holidays and the long weekends. Book early for those dates.

Do you publish prices?

No. Each booking is priced on the program, the dates and the number of passengers. You get a price the same business day, Argentina time.
