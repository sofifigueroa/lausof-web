The service

Cold chain transport in Salta and northwest Argentina

We move frozen and chilled goods at controlled temperature from origin to destination. The cold chain is not broken on any leg: the cargo travels in refrigerated trucks with a refrigeration unit and is delivered at the temperature it requires.

The temperature is logged during the trip. If your customer or your quality team asks for a record of how the goods traveled, you have it.

We do the whole leg or only the part you need: pickup, storage, consolidation or distribution. And because the refrigeration units are maintained in our own workshop, the trucks leave for the road in working order.

Warehouse

Cold room storage in Salta

We have our own warehouse in Salta with a cold room, a truck yard and truck access. Companies in the region, in sectors such as mining and catering, have been storing goods with us for years, both chilled and ambient.

The cold room is our own, inside the warehouse, and it is arranged around what your operation needs: cold positions on demand for storage, consolidation of cargo from different origins, order preparation and staging, and cross-docking, where goods arrive, are prepared and go straight out for delivery without being stored. We also handle distribution in the city and the region.

You do not have to contract the whole service: you can use only the storage, only the distribution, or the leg you are missing. Tell us how your operation works and we will put the scheme together.

Frequently asked questions

What buyers ask us before we quote

What kinds of cargo do you carry?

Frozen and chilled goods at controlled temperature, hanging half carcasses in the insulated body with rails, and general freight without refrigeration: curtain-side, box body and flatbed truck.

How do I know what temperature my cargo traveled at?

The temperature is logged during the trip. There is a record of how the goods traveled, for your customer or your quality team.

Do you reach destinations on gravel roads or at high altitude?

Yes. For destinations a conventional refrigerated truck cannot reach, we have a 4x4 refrigerated truck that holds the cold chain on gravel and at altitude.

Can I use the warehouse without the freight?

Yes. The cold room storage can be used on its own: storage, consolidation or distribution, depending on what your operation needs.
