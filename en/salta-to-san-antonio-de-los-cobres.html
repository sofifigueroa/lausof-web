The route

Route 51 through the Quebrada del Toro

It is about 164 km (102 miles) from the city of Salta on National Route 51, paved all the way. Without stops the drive takes about three hours. The road passes the airport and Campo Quijano, climbs through the Quebrada del Toro, continues past Santa Rosa de Tastil and crosses the Abra Blanca pass at about 4,080 m (13,400 ft) before dropping to San Antonio de los Cobres at about 3,775 m (12,400 ft).

Altogether the road climbs almost 2,900 m (9,500 ft). That is why we drive it with stops: the body copes better with a gradual climb.

La Polvorilla

The viaduct, by road

The La Polvorilla viaduct is about 16 to 18 km beyond San Antonio de los Cobres on Ruta 40. It is 224 m long and 63 m high, above 4,200 m (13,800 ft). You reach it by minibus or pickup and see it from below and from the viewpoints.

On October 9, 2026, the national road agency listed Ruta 40 between La Poma and San Antonio de los Cobres as open, with machines working throughout the area. We check the road before every departure.

Tren a las Nubes

If your trip includes the train

The Tren a las Nubes reaches the La Polvorilla viaduct and runs all year. Lausof is an independent transport company: we do not operate the train or sell its tickets. Schedules, fares and tickets are on the official Tren a las Nubes website. The operator notes that it is a high-mountain excursion that can be cancelled for weather.

If your itinerary combines the train with a leg by road, we arrange the road transfer around the times the operator confirms to you.

Frequently asked questions

What travelers ask before booking

Is the road to San Antonio de los Cobres paved?

Yes. Route 51 is paved from Salta to San Antonio de los Cobres. The stretch on to the La Polvorilla viaduct, on Ruta 40, is a narrower road.

How long does it take?

About three hours of driving without stops, for roughly 164 km. Because we climb with stops, the trip up takes longer. That is an estimate.

Can we go and come back in one day?

Yes. It is a full day out of Salta, with the viaduct included if you want. It can also be the first leg of a longer trip into the Puna.

How bad is the altitude?

San Antonio de los Cobres is at about 3,775 m (12,400 ft) and the road goes above 4,000 m (13,000 ft). Most people feel it on the first day. We climb slowly, we stop, and if someone is not doing well we turn back.

Do you take people to the Tren a las Nubes?

We do not operate or sell the train. If your itinerary includes it, we arrange the road leg around the times the train operator confirms to you.

Can we continue to Chile?

San Antonio de los Cobres is on the road to the international crossing toward San Pedro de Atacama, in Chile, and we run international trips. Ask us with your dates. For work in the mining areas, see mining logistics.
