How it works

The minibus stays with your group, not just for one ride

A transfer takes the group from one place to another and leaves. With a minibus at your disposal, the vehicle and the driver stay with the group for the day or the days you book: they leave when the group wants, wait at every stop and follow the route agreed beforehand.

It works for a day with several stops around Salta city, for a full-day trip to the valleys or the Quebrada, and for multi-day journeys with nights away, where the same minibus and the same driver go with the group from town to town.

You or your agency set the route. If you do not have one yet, we tell you what fits in a day for the road and the season, using the distances on our route pages.

Questions

What people ask before hiring a minibus with driver

What is the difference between a transfer and a minibus at our disposal?

A transfer takes the group from one place to another. With the minibus at your disposal, the vehicle and the driver stay with the group for the agreed day or days and handle every ride.

How many hours does a day include?

It depends on the route. We agree on it when we price the trip, with the schedule for each day, and it is written into the quote.

Can we change the plan during the trip?

Talk it over with the driver and with us by WhatsApp. Changes that add many kilometers or hours are agreed before they are made.

Is the driver a guide?

The driver knows the roads and the stops. If you want a licensed tour guide, we can arrange one: mention it when you ask for the quote.

Do the drivers speak English?

Our drivers work in Spanish. An English-speaking driver can be arranged on request, subject to availability. Messages in English are answered in English by email or WhatsApp.

Do you publish prices?

No. Each booking is priced on the days, the route and the number of passengers. You get a price the same business day, Argentina time.
