The warehouse

Our own warehouse in the city of Salta

Our warehouse is a building we own, about 3,000 m² in the city of Salta, with a truck yard and truck access: freight comes in, is moved and goes out without depending on the street. Our in-house workshop runs on the same site, so the place works every day, with our own people to receive and dispatch.

Companies in the region have trusted us with their goods for years, from mining-sector suppliers to catering contractors. We store, consolidate and dispatch according to how each operation works.

What you can do here

Storage, consolidation and cross-docking in Salta

The warehouse is used whole or in parts: tell us what your operation needs and we put the arrangement together.

Storage by period

Goods, supplies or equipment kept for weeks or months.

Consolidation and staging

We receive freight from different origins and leave it ready to dispatch together.

Cross-docking

Your freight arrives at the warehouse and leaves on our own fleet: distribution in Salta or haulage to the Puna.

Operating base

Yard space for the units and our in-house workshop on the same site.

The difference from renting an empty warehouse is that there is a transport company behind this one: what comes into the warehouse can leave on our own fleet, toward Salta, the valleys or the Puna, without coordinating with a third party.

If you are setting us up as a supplier, tell us which certificates the registration asks for. We can send our CNRT license No. 60284 (Argentina's national road-transport regulator, interprovincial passenger and freight), our AMT permit (Salta's provincial transport authority), proof that cargo and passenger insurance is in force, and our tax registration.

Cold

Cold positions available on request

Inside the warehouse we have our own cold room. Cold positions are opened on request: tell us what the goods are, what volume and for how long, and we put the proposal together. Minimums and terms to be agreed.

Do you also need the transport under temperature? We run refrigerated freight in Salta and northwest Argentina, with the cold chain from origin to destination, so the same supplier covers both the storage and the trip.

Frequently asked questions

What buyers ask before we quote

Can space be rented by period?

Yes. Space is contracted for the period you need, weeks or months, under contract. Tell us what you are going to store and how much room you need, and we send you the proposal.

Do you receive and consolidate goods?

Yes. We receive your freight in the truck yard with our own people, store it or consolidate it with other freight, and dispatch it, including on our own fleet, toward Salta or the Puna.

Do you issue Factura A for companies?

Yes. We are Lausof S.R.L., CUIT (Argentine tax ID) 30-71146774-9, and we issue Factura A, the Argentine tax invoice. No call center: someone from the company answers.

Do you reply in English?

Yes, by email and WhatsApp. Calls to the published line are answered in Spanish.

Looking for warehouse space or a base in Salta?

Tell us what you need to store or move and for how long. We reply with a written proposal.
