Mining logistics and high-altitude operations

More than a decade working in the Puna: crew to mine camps, cargo to the salt flats and on-road mechanical support above 4,000 m (13,000 ft). We know the mine-site entry requirements and the road from Salta to San Antonio de los Cobres, Pocitos and Tolar Grande.

  • Crew transport to mine camps and shift rotations
  • Cargo and equipment to lithium projects on the salt flats of Salta and Jujuy
  • 4x4 pickups fitted out for mine-site entry: roll cage, buggy whip, strobe beacon, fire extinguishers, a second spare wheel and snow chains
  • Satellite phone on board on high-altitude jobs
  • On-road mechanical support with our own crew and equipment

About us

A family-owned company from Salta

Lausof is a family-owned company based in Salta. We started in 2010 with passenger transport and in 2017 added towing, roadside assistance and general freight. Since then we have done all of it: school transport, day trips, distribution, cold chain and mining work in the Puna.

Today we focus on a handful of services we know how to do well: towing, personnel, cold chain and the workshop. They are run by the same people as always, drivers and mechanics who know the units and the roads by heart.

The phone is answered by someone from the company, not a call center.
