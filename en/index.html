Why work with us

Fewer parties involved, fewer things to go wrong

In-house fleet and workshop

We repair our own units with our own mechanics. Less time off the road, and fewer surprises for the client.

High-altitude experience

Gravel roads, salt flats and camps above 4,000 m (13,000 ft). We know what it takes to get there and, above all, to get back.

A family company

No call center: questions are answered by the company itself.

Based in Salta

We have worked in Salta and northwest Argentina since 2010, from the provincial capital to the Puna.

Safety

Safety is the condition for the work, not an extra

When you carry people, or enter a mine site, safety is what makes the work possible at all. These are the practices we hold to every day.

Units fitted out for mine-site entry

Buggy whip, strobe beacon, fire extinguishers, a second spare wheel and snow chains on the units assigned to that work.

On-board trip log per unit

Every trip is recorded.

Incident reports signed by the driver

Every incident is documented, without exception.

Zero tolerance

Zero alcohol and no drugs at the wheel. Smoke-free vehicles.

Documented preventive maintenance

In our own workshop, not subcontracted.

Drivers with a professional license

Current medical fitness certificate (psicofísico) and workers' compensation insurance (ART) in force.

Insurance in force

Third-party liability and cargo or passenger cover, depending on the service.

About us

A family-owned company from Salta

Lausof is a family-owned company based in Salta. We started in 2010 with passenger transport and in 2017 added general freight. Since then we have done all of it: school transport, day trips, distribution, cold chain and mining work in the Puna.

Today our core is passenger transport (tours, mining and corporate crews, airport transfers and charters) and refrigerated freight to the mines, alongside the workshop. They are run by the same people as always, drivers and mechanics who know the units and the roads by heart.
