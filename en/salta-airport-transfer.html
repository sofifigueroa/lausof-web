How to get there

How to get from Salta airport to downtown

Martín Miguel de Güemes international airport sits on National Route 51, about 11 to 13 km (7 to 8 miles) from downtown Salta: around 20 minutes by car without traffic. From the airport into town you can take a taxi or remis, the city bus, or a transfer booked in advance.

OptionWhat to know
Distance and timeAbout 11 to 13 km to the downtown hotels, depending on the hotel, and around 20 minutes without traffic.
RoadNational Route 51 into the city. Paved.
Taxi or remisTaxis and remises (private cars with a driver) wait at the airport, no booking needed.
By busThe SAETA city bus links the airport with the city. It takes longer than a car because of the stops.
Private transfer with LausofA 19-seat minibus or 4x4 pickup with a driver, for your group and its luggage, timed to your flight; also straight to Cafayate, Purmamarca or Cachi. We normally reply with a price the same business day.

Location: argentina.gob.ar; distances: Google Maps and OSRM (see below). Taxis, remises and bus: Rome2Rio and Omio, checked Nov 3, 2026.

Airport to downtown

About 11 to 13 km and around 20 minutes to the hotels in town

Martín Miguel de Güemes international airport sits on National Route 51, at kilometer 5, southwest of the city. Plaza 9 de Julio and the downtown hotels are about 11 to 13 km away on paved road, depending on the hotel: around 20 minutes without traffic, longer at rush hour.

Send us the flight number, the date and arrival time, how many are traveling, how much luggage you carry and the address where you are staying. With the flight number we follow the actual arrival time, and we confirm the meeting point at the airport by WhatsApp or email before you fly.

It works the other way too: we pick you up at the hotel and drop you at the airport in good time for your departure.

Airport location from argentina.gob.ar ("Cómo llegar a Salta", checked November 3, 2026). Distances and times are approximate, by car without stops: Google Maps (October 10, 2026) and OpenStreetMap with the OSRM route planner (November 3, 2026).

Questions

What travelers ask before booking an airport transfer

How long is the ride from Salta airport to downtown?

About 11 to 13 km depending on the hotel, around 20 minutes without traffic. At rush hour it can take longer.

What if my flight is delayed?

That is why we ask for the flight number: with it we follow the actual arrival time. If the schedule changes a lot, we coordinate with you by WhatsApp or email.

Can you take us from the airport straight to Cafayate, Purmamarca or Cachi?

Yes. The minibus leaves the airport straight for your destination, without going into town, and stops on the way if you need it.

Do you also drive from the hotel to the airport?

Yes, both ways: on arrival and for your departure, from Salta or from the town where you are staying.

Do the drivers speak English?

Our drivers work in Spanish. An English-speaking driver can be arranged on request, subject to availability. Messages in English are answered in English by email or WhatsApp.

Do you publish prices?

No. Each ride is priced on the date, the time, the number of passengers, the luggage and the destination. You get a price the same business day, Argentina time.
