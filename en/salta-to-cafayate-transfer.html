The route

Salta to Cafayate on Ruta 68

It is about 190 km (118 miles) from the city of Salta, all on National Route 68 and paved the whole way. Without stops the drive takes about two and a half hours. The road crosses the Lerma Valley through Cerrillos, El Carril, Coronel Moldes and La Viña, and at Alemania it enters the Quebrada de las Conchas: about 80 km of red rock formations down to Cafayate.

The classic stops are in the gorge: the Garganta del Diablo, the Anfiteatro (a natural amphitheater with acoustics you can test on the spot), Los Castillos and the dunes just before Cafayate. The driver stops wherever you want, as long as the road allows it safely.

Cafayate sits above 1,600 m (5,200 ft). That is not Puna altitude, but the sun and the dry air are strong: bring water, sunscreen and a layer for the evening.

Ways to book

One way, a day trip, or several days

One way

From your hotel, the bus terminal or Salta airport to your lodging in Cafayate, or the reverse. We choose the vehicle for the group and the luggage.

Day trip

An early start, stops in the gorge, lunch and wineries in Cafayate, and back to Salta the same day. The driver stays with the group all day.

Several days

The minibus stays with you: Cafayate, San Carlos, Angastaco and Cachi on Ruta 40, and back to Salta over the Cuesta del Obispo. Details on Salta to Cachi and Ruta 40.

From the airport

From Salta's Martín Miguel de Güemes airport (SLA) straight to Cafayate. Send the flight number and we set the pickup time around it.

Wineries

Cafayate's wineries, with nobody behind the wheel

Cafayate is the center of Salta's high-altitude wine route: Torrontés, Malbec, Cabernet Sauvignon, Tannat and other varieties, large estates and small family wineries, and the Museo de la Vid y el Vino. You choose and book the tastings with each winery, or your travel agency does. We drive you between them and back to your hotel or to Salta.

Salta province has a zero-tolerance rule for alcohol at the wheel. With a driver, nobody in the group has to drive after the tasting.

Frequently asked questions

What travelers ask before booking

How long is the drive from Salta to Cafayate?

About two and a half hours without stops, for roughly 190 km. With stops in the Quebrada de las Conchas, plan on three to four hours. That is an estimate: it depends on how often you want to stop.

Is the road paved?

Yes. Ruta 68 is paved all the way from Salta to Cafayate. Gravel only starts if the trip continues north to Cachi on Ruta 40.

Can we go and come back in one day?

Yes, it is the most requested day trip out of Salta. You are back in Salta in the late afternoon or evening, depending on the start time and how long you spend at the wineries.

Do the drivers speak English?

Our drivers work in Spanish. An English-speaking driver can be arranged on request, subject to availability. Messages in English are answered in English by email or WhatsApp.

Can you take us from Cafayate to Salta airport?

Yes, in both directions. Send the flight time and we work out when to leave Cafayate.

Do you publish prices?

No. Each trip is priced on the date, the number of passengers, the vehicle and whether it is one way, a day trip or several days. You get a price the same business day, Argentina time.
