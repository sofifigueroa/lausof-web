How to get there

How to get from San Pedro de Atacama to Salta

San Pedro de Atacama to Salta is about 590 km (365 miles) on Ruta 27 CH, the Jama Pass and Ruta 52, paved all the way. Driving time without stops is close to 8 hours; with the border check and a few stops it takes a full day. You can do it on a scheduled bus or with a private transfer.

The road

San Pedro to Salta, leg by leg

In this direction the trip starts with a climb. San Pedro de Atacama sits at about 2,500 m (8,200 ft) and the Jama border complex at 4,200 m (13,800 ft), some two hours up Ruta 27 CH. Then comes the Jujuy plateau on Ruta 52, the descent to Purmamarca, and Ruta 9 on to Salta.

Pure driving time is close to 8 hours. Add the border check, the stops and the slower pace the altitude asks for: we leave early so we reach Jama with time to spare.

The border

The Jama Pass, heading into Argentina

Jama is an integrated border complex on the Argentine side, where both countries work in the same building. Coming from Chile, you exit Chile and enter Argentina in a single stop: immigration, customs and the Argentine food and agriculture inspection (SENASA).

The two governments publish different hours, and the hours change with the season. On Nov 3, 2026, Argentina's official page listed 9:00 to 19:15 and Chile's listed 8:00 to 20:00, Chilean time. From September to April mainland Chile shares Argentina's clock; the rest of the year it is one hour behind. We time the departure from San Pedro to reach Jama with a margin, and check the hours the day before.

The pass closes for snow and blizzards, in winter and in summer too. In August 2026 it was closed from the 11th for snowfall, whiteout winds and sub-zero temperatures, with stretches of Ruta 27 CH impassable. There were closures in January as well, during the Altiplano summer storms. If the pass is closed on your travel day, we tell you before we leave and move the date with you.

Live status: Jama Pass on argentina.gob.ar (Border Police, National Roads and Weather Service) and the daily status of Chile's border complexes (hours last updated there on Apr 7, 2026). August 2026 closure: El Tribuno de Jujuy, Aug 22, 2026. Checked Nov 3, 2026.

Documents and food rules

What each passenger needs to enter Argentina

  • Most travelers from outside South America: a valid passport. Depending on nationality, a tourist visa may be needed, issued by an Argentine consulate before the trip.
  • Argentine citizens: national ID card (DNI) or passport.
  • Citizens of Mercosur and associated countries (for example Chile, Brazil, Uruguay, Paraguay, Bolivia, Peru, Colombia, Ecuador): the national ID card recognized under the Mercosur agreement, or a passport, unless their nationality needs a visa.
  • Minors leaving Chile who do not travel with both parents need the authorization of the parent who is not traveling; the Chilean police (PDI) check it at the border. Ask a Chilean consulate about your case before the trip.

Argentine food rules (SENASA). On entering Argentina, SENASA checks the food you carry and can confiscate what is not allowed. Fresh fruit and vegetables, meat of any kind, cured sausages, honey, homemade food and unlabeled food are not allowed in. Factory-packed products such as canned food, jams, roasted or salted nuts and some dairy products are allowed in amounts for personal use. Our advice: bring no fresh food.

Official sources, checked Nov 3, 2026: Mercosur travel documents (Argentine immigration), tourist visa (Argentine Foreign Ministry), travel authorization for minors (Government of Chile) and entering Argentina (SENASA). Rules change: confirm yours with your consulate before you travel.

Pickup in San Pedro

Where we pick you up and what you can add

The vehicle drives up from Salta to collect you, so we agree the day, time and place well ahead: your lodging in San Pedro de Atacama or another point in town. It is a private transfer, so the stops are yours to choose.

  • One way: we pick you up in San Pedro and drop you at your hotel in Salta, the bus terminal, or Salta or Jujuy airport.
  • With stops or a night on the way: Salinas Grandes, the Cuesta de Lipán and Purmamarca are on the road. If you would rather sleep in Purmamarca and continue the next day, we plan it.
  • Combined trips: if you are coming from the Uyuni salt flat or touring Chile and Bolivia, we plan the leg to Salta as part of a longer trip. See also the Uyuni to Salta transfer.

Altitude. Going from about 2,500 m to 4,200 m in a couple of hours is noticeable, even after days in the desert. Drink water and eat light. If you have a medical condition, ask your doctor before the trip.

Frequently asked questions

What travelers ask before crossing into Argentina

How long does San Pedro de Atacama to Salta take?

Driving time is close to 8 hours for about 590 km. With the border check at Jama and stops it is a full day, so we leave San Pedro early to cross in daylight.

Is there a bus from San Pedro de Atacama to Salta?

Yes. Several companies run it on fixed days and times, arriving at the Salta bus terminal. A private transfer leaves on the day and at the time you choose, picks you up at your lodging and stops wherever your group wants.

What happens if the Jama Pass is closed?

We check the pass the day before and again before leaving. If it is closed for snow or wind, we do not set off: we let you know and find the nearest date when the pass is open.

What food can I bring into Argentina?

The easiest choice is to bring no fresh food. SENASA does not allow fresh fruit and vegetables, meat, cured sausages, honey or homemade food, and can confiscate them at the border. Factory-packed products such as canned food or jams are allowed in amounts for personal use.

Do I need a visa for Argentina?

It depends on your nationality. Citizens of Mercosur and associated countries enter with their ID card. Everyone else enters with a passport, and some nationalities need a tourist visa from an Argentine consulate before the trip.

Do you publish prices?

No. The price depends on the date, the number of travelers, one way or return, and the vehicle the group needs. Send us those details and we reply with a price.
