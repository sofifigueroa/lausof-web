Carnival 2027

Quebrada de Humahuaca carnival dates, 2027

The carnival of the Quebrada is the biggest celebration of the year in Jujuy: comparsas (carnival troupes) dancing through the villages, music in the streets, and the ritual unearthing of the carnival devil that opens it. Monday 8 and Tuesday 9 February are Argentine national holidays, so the main carnival falls on a four-day weekend.

Getting there

Flying in and driving up Ruta 9

The nearest airports are Salta (SLA) and Jujuy (JUJ). From Salta the road goes through San Salvador de Jujuy, Yala and Volcán; Purmamarca is a short detour on Ruta 52, and Maimará, Tilcara and Humahuaca are further up Ruta 9. Driving times below are car estimates; a minibus on carnival traffic takes longer.

Distances from Salta city
  • Purmamarca — 179 km (111 mi), about 2 h 15 by car (estimate)
  • Tilcara — 197 km (122 mi), about 2 h 30 by car (estimate)
  • Humahuaca — 239 km (149 mi), about 3 h by car (estimate)
  • Jujuy airport (JUJ) to Tilcara — about 115 km (71 mi)
Carnival traffic

On the Saturday of the 2026 carnival (February 14), Ruta 9 had long lines heading north from Yala toward the Quebrada (Todo Jujuy). We plan an early departure with time to spare.

As of October 10, 2026, no road closures have been announced for the 2027 carnival. If any are, we will add them here with their source.

Where to stay and how to get back

When the Quebrada is full, plan the return before you leave

During the 2026 carnival hotel occupancy reached 98% in the Quebrada, 98.9% in Tilcara and 98.2% in Purmamarca, and 97.6% in San Salvador de Jujuy, according to the Jujuy Ministry of Culture and Tourism. Many visitors sleep in Salta and go up each day. Three ways to set up the trip:

Nightly round trip

You leave Salta, the driver waits in the village and the return starts at the time you agreed. A good fit if you could not find a room in the Quebrada.

Vehicle at your disposal

The minibus and driver stay with your group through the main carnival and drive you between your lodging and each night's village.

Drop-off and pickup

If you already have lodging in the Quebrada, we take you there with your luggage on the first day, from Salta airport if you are flying in, and collect you on the last.

A fixed meeting point

The return time and place are written down in the chat before you leave. If someone is running late, the group lets us know on the same thread.

Your group and the vehicle

19-seat minibuses with a professional driver

Nineteen passengers plus the driver. Larger groups get a quote for extra vehicles on the same trip. Luggage for several days travels in the same minibus. Our drivers work in Spanish; an English-speaking driver can be requested, subject to availability.

Frequently asked questions

What visitors ask about the carnival

Can we go to one village one night and another the next?

Yes, with the vehicle at your disposal. We set a plan per night (Tilcara on Saturday and Humahuaca on Sunday, for example) and the driver takes you between your lodging and each village.

Can you pick us up at the airport?

Yes, at Salta (SLA) or Jujuy (JUJ). Send the flight number and arrival time, and the minibus takes you straight to the Quebrada or to your lodging.

Do you sell carnival tickets or packages?

No. We provide the transport only. The celebrations belong to each town and its comparsas; anything that requires a ticket is booked with its organizer.

Can we coordinate in English?

Yes. Write to us by email or WhatsApp in English and we reply in English, normally the same business day (Argentina time, UTC−3). Phone calls are answered in Spanish.

Do you publish prices?

No. The price depends on the days, the village and whether the vehicle stays with you. Send those details and you get a price for the whole vehicle.
