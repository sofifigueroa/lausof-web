Privacy

In force since November 7, 2026

This is a translation. The Spanish text is the one that applies: Privacidad.

Who is responsible

Data received through this website, WhatsApp, phone or email is kept in a database of LAUSOF S.R.L. (CUIT 30-71146774-9), whose registered office is at Los Perales 22, Salta. Requests about personal data go to transporte@lausof.com.

What data is received and why

Visits. The site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies in the browser and records, as statistics, which pages are visited, from which country and on what device, and where the visit came from. This shows which pages are useful. How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Inquiries. What you write by WhatsApp or email, or say by phone, is used to reply, to quote and to provide the service.

Only what is needed to quote or book is asked for. Giving it is voluntary. Without it no quote or booking can be prepared, and with incorrect data the quote or booking may not match what was asked for.

Who receives it

  • Google: Google Analytics and email.
  • Netlify: hosts the site and logs each request to the server, with the IP address.
  • Apacheta: the management system that receives, on Lausof’s behalf, the emails.

Google, Netlify and Apacheta handle the data in the United States and other countries.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to transporte@lausof.com. Access requests are answered within 10 calendar days, and corrections or deletions within 5 business days.
