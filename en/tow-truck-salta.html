The service

Hookup, transport and delivery wherever you tell us

We recover and move vehicles on a hydraulic flatbed tow truck: the vehicle rides on the deck instead of rolling, also when it is damaged or will not start. We handle it end to end: hookup, transport and delivery at the workshop, the dealer or the address you name.

We work for private owners, insurers and company fleets. Besides emergency towing, we move vehicles on a scheduled date: a car to the workshop, a fleet vehicle to another city or a machine to a work site.

  • Flatbed towing on a hydraulic deck
  • Cars, pickups, minibuses, road machinery and damaged vehicles
  • On call 24 hours a day, every day of the year
  • Mechanical help on site, with mechanics from our own workshop
  • Recoveries far from anywhere: we carry our own welder and generator
  • Scheduled vehicle transport within Salta and along the highways of northwest Argentina
  • Remito (Argentine goods-in-transit note) and Factura A for insurers and fleets

Roadside help in the Puna

A vehicle stopped in the Puna cannot wait for someone to improvise a rescue

We have worked in the Puna for more than a decade, for mining companies and for people driving its roads. We provide mechanical help above 4,000 meters with our own crew and equipment: we go up with mechanics from our workshop, a welder and a generator, and with the flatbed tow truck when the vehicle has to be brought down.

On high-altitude jobs we carry a satellite phone on board. A recovery in the Puna is planned as a trip of its own, so we ask for your exact location, the vehicle and what happened, and confirm by phone or WhatsApp how we will handle it.

  • RN 51 to San Antonio de los Cobres and RN 52 to the Salinas Grandes
  • Rental 4x4 pickups, overland vehicles and private cars stranded on the road
  • Mining company and contractor vehicles on the salt flats: mining logistics
  • If the vehicle cannot be fixed on the spot, we bring it down on the flatbed

How to request a tow

Call or write with these three details

Where you are

The address, or the route number and kilometer marker. On WhatsApp you can share your phone's location.

The vehicle

What it is (car, pickup, minibus, machine), with the make and model.

What happened

Whether it will not start, had an accident or is off the road. And where it should go.

Scheduled transport

To move a vehicle on a set date (to a workshop, another city or a work site), you get a price the same business day, with remito and Factura A.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask before calling a tow truck

Do you work at night, on weekends and on holidays?

Yes. Towing and roadside assistance are on call 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Call +54 9 387 526-9009.

Which vehicles can you tow?

Cars, pickups, minibuses, road machinery and damaged vehicles, on a hydraulic flatbed. If you are unsure about a particular vehicle, send us the make and model and we will confirm.

Do you go outside Salta city?

Yes: to the Lerma Valley, the highways of northwest Argentina, mountain access roads and the Puna. For a roadside recovery, give us the route number and the kilometer marker.

My rental car broke down. Can you help?

Yes, we tow rental cars and rental 4x4 pickups. Tell us where you are and where the vehicle should go, and let your rental company know as well.

Can you fix the vehicle on the spot?

When it can be done, yes: we come with mechanics from our own workshop, a welder and a generator. If it cannot be fixed there, we take it on the flatbed.

How much does a tow cost?

We do not publish prices: every job is priced by location, distance and vehicle. In an emergency, call and we arrange it over the phone. For scheduled transport, you get a price the same business day.
