The construction phase

Building a plant moves more people than running it

While a plant is being built on a salt flat, the headcount changes every week: mechanical, civil and electrical crews come in, along with support staff, each on its own rotation, and many of them fly in from other provinces. Transport has to keep pace without anyone left waiting in Salta or at the camp.

We work for the contractors, EPCs and service companies that put those crews together. We provide 19-seat minibuses with a professional driver and 4x4 pickups for supervisors, backed by the same decade-plus of driving up to the Puna described on mining logistics.

Shifts and peaks

Shift changes, staffing peaks and fly-in crews

Shift changes

Crews up and down on each project’s rotation, with on-time departures.

Staffing peaks

When the project adds crews, we add vehicles to the same shift change and tell you early what we can cover.

Fly-in crews

We meet crews at Salta airport or the bus terminal and take them up to camp the same day.

24 hours

Pre-dawn departures, late-night arrivals and weekend runs: we are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Supervisors and site visits

4x4 pickups with a driver for site managers, inspections and technical visits, or on a monthly rental for your company.

Backup on the road

Our own workshop and mechanics who go out to a unit stopped in the Puna; if it has to be moved, we coordinate the transfer.

Camps and routes

Every lithium area of the Puna, in all three provinces

We drive to every mining area and route of the Puna. These are the salt flats and the access roads used most; if your project sits elsewhere on the plateau, we reach it too.

Vendor onboarding

The paperwork the gate and procurement ask for

  • Lausof S.R.L., CUIT 30-71146774-9, invoicing with Factura A
  • CNRT license No. 60284 for interprovincial trips and an AMT permit within Salta
  • Passenger insurance in force on every unit
  • Drivers licensed for the vehicle class, with a current medical fitness certificate (psicofísico) and workers’ compensation insurance (ART)
  • Units with a current technical inspection, maintained in our own workshop
  • 4x4 pickups equipped for mine-site entry: roll cage, buggy whip, strobe beacon, fire extinguishers, a second spare wheel and snow chains
  • An on-board trip log for each unit: every trip is on record

If your safety or procurement team uses its own form, send it to us and we will fill it in with the documents for each unit and each driver.

How to get a quote

What we need to quote crew transport for your project

The project

The salt flat or the camp, and where the crews start from: Salta city, the airport, San Antonio de los Cobres or Jujuy.

The shifts

The rotation, the shift-change days and the departure and arrival times.

The headcount

How many people go up per shift, and how you expect that to grow at the project’s peaks.

Our reply

A written quote the same business day (Argentina time, UTC−3), invoiced with Factura A.

Frequently asked questions

What contractors ask before they hire us

Can you cover a staffing peak?

Yes. We add vehicles to the same shift change. The earlier we know when the project ramps up, the better we can plan vehicles and drivers.

Do you run at night and on weekends?

Yes. We are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, because shift changes and flights do not keep office hours.

Do you go to Catamarca and Jujuy?

Yes. From the Salar del Hombre Muerto to Olaroz and Cauchari: interprovincial trips run under our CNRT license No. 60284.

How do you stay in touch where there is no cell signal?

Units in service carry GPS, and on high-altitude runs a satellite phone and Starlink satellite internet travel on board.

Can you also move cargo and equipment to the site?

Yes: general cargo and refrigerated cargo. We also coordinate vehicle transfers for companies and mining operations, quoted on request. See mining logistics in the Puna.

Do you speak English?

Emails and WhatsApp messages in English are answered in English. Our drivers work in Spanish; phone calls are answered in Spanish.
